Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

KeyMaster is designed with privacy at its core. Keyword queries, search history, and optional user-provided Google Gemini API keys are saved exclusively in your browser's local client storage (localStorage). We do not collect or sell your personal search queries to third parties.

2. API Keys & Local Storage

If you choose to enter your own Google Gemini API key in the settings modal, it is stored locally on your device to authenticate direct requests to Google APIs. You can clear this key at any time by clearing your browser cache or updating the settings modal.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We use essential web performance performance indicators to monitor uptime and aggregate usage metrics. No personal identifying information (PII) is stored.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy policy, please contact privacy@keymaster-seo.com.
