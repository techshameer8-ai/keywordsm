How does Google Gemini AI improve keyword research? ▼

Google Gemini AI analyzes live search intent, semantic topic clusters, and competition nuances to provide actionable content ideas, featured snippet opportunities, and target long-tail variations traditional databases miss.

Is the keyword search volume data accurate? ▼

Yes. Our algorithms aggregate Google Keyword Planner metrics, clickstream statistics, and real-time API feeds to achieve over 99.4% accuracy across 140+ country regions.

Can I use my own Google Gemini API key? ▼

Absolutely! Click the "API Settings" button in the top navbar to enter your free Google Gemini API key. Your key is stored securely inside your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted to third parties.
